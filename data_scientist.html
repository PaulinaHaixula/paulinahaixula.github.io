Research Findings: Hair Loss & Condition Analysis

Early-stage breakage (Class 1 and Class 2) was the most common outcome across all race groups. Black/African participants showed higher structural compromise, consistent with trichological studies indicating irregular cuticle layers.

White participants exhibited the highest proportion of dry hair (53.1%) but least damage. Asian participants showed moderate healthy hair levels. Black/African participants had higher damage (9.1%), reflecting hair fragility. Mixed-race participants demonstrated the highest damage levels (28.6%).
